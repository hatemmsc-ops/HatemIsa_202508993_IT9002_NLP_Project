## C. CEO deck findings

1. All growth percentages on Slides 2 and 3 recalculate correctly from their stated bases (see Section F).
2. Slide 3's row label "III. AVERAGE INVESTMENT PER CLIENT" did not match the board deck's "III. AVERAGE DEPOSIT PER CLIENT" for the identical metric. **Fixed.**
3. Slide 15's "Views" and "Interactions" tiles are all-time cumulative figures, sitting directly above a "Top Performing Content" table of per-post, more recent figures. Not wrong, but the mixed timeframe was unlabeled. **Fixed** — tiles now read "Views (all-time)" / "Interactions (all-time)".
4. Slide 18's four traffic-source rows sum to 98.6% of sessions (579 of 587), not 100%, because 8 sessions fall outside GA's top-source breakdown. This was previously unexplained. **Fixed** — added a footnote.
5. Slide 16's LinkedIn followers figure (531) is lower than the deck's previous figure (586). This is a real drop or a measurement-basis change; no source file explains which. **Not fixed — flagged as open**, since fabricating an explanation would violate the no-invention rule.
6. The "793% week-over-week active users" claim (Slide 9) is an accurate transcription of the GA screenshot. Its attribution to "the Saudi test launch" is a reasonable, evidenced inference (Google Ads launched 22 Sep in Saudi/Riyadh; the spike window is 21–27 Sep; paid traffic is 69.7% of sessions in the same period) but is not a screenshot-proven causal statement. The deck's wording ("...following the Saudi test launch") uses temporal, not causal, language, which is appropriately hedged. No change made.
7. Ali Sabeel's "Completed 18 Sep" status is inferred from the absence of any further Ignite invoice for that asset after TI7514 (13 Aug). This is a reasonable inference, not a direct confirmation from Ignite that the campaign formally closed. The deck's own note ("no further spend invoiced") already discloses this basis. No change made, flagged for awareness.
8. Google Ads targeting detail ("Saudi Arabia and Riyadh interest, Saudi residents only") is sourced from your direct statement in this session, not a file. Flagged as a different evidence class; not removed, since a direct principal statement is valid.
9. The board deck has **no sponsored-ads slide at all** — it never carried the "Sponsored Ads: What's Live Now" content that exists in the CEO deck. The brief's hypothesis that "board deck slide 9 may still show older sponsored-ads wording" is incorrect: board deck Slide 9 is the KPI appendix table, an entirely different slide. No cross-deck marketing wording conflict exists because the board deck carries no marketing-status wording to conflict with.
